# TryLocal – Online Discovery, Offline Certainty

**Discover online. Try locally. Buy with confidence.**

TryLocal reduces fashion returns by enabling **online discovery and offline trials** through integration with **local retail stores**.

## Problem Statement
### Sustainability & Circular Fashion

Fashion e-commerce currently suffers from extremely high return rates caused by:
- Size mismatch  
- Fit uncertainty  
- Product expectation gaps  

### These returns lead to:
- Heavy reverse logistics costs  
- Increased environmental impact  
- Poor customer experience  
- Declining local retailer visibility and footfall  
- Large volumes of dead inventory  

Existing e-commerce models focus on delivery speed while ignoring post-purchase inefficiencies, making them economically and environmentally unsustainable.

---

## Solution Overview
TryLocal bridges the gap between **online convenience** and **offline certainty**.

Instead of shipping products to customers and handling returns, TryLocal enables:
- Online discovery  
- Offline trial  
- In-store purchase  

This approach eliminates unnecessary returns while empowering local fashion retailers with digital visibility.

---

## Brief Project Description
TryLocal is a **hyperlocal fashion discovery platform** that connects online browsing with offline retail experiences. Users can explore styles, sizes, and availability digitally, then visit nearby stores to try products before making a purchase.

## Challenges and Mitigation Strategies

Introducing TryLocal involves adoption challenges such as consumer preference for doorstep delivery, inconsistent inventory management, pricing variability, and uncertain purchase intent. The platform is designed for incremental adoption, transparency, and flexibility to adapt to real-world retail behavior.

### 1. Inventory Accuracy
**Challenge:**  
Local retailers often lack real-time inventory systems.

**Mitigation:**  
TryLocal uses a simple three-status inventory system:
- In Stock  
- Out of Stock  
- Restocked  

Availability is communicated through confidence levels rather than strict live synchronization. User feedback, automatic hiding of inaccurate listings, assisted onboarding, periodic inventory checks, and trust mechanisms such as accuracy badges and ranking incentives ensure reliability.

---

### 2. No Guaranteed Purchase
**Challenge:**  
In-store trials may not always result in purchases.

**Mitigation:**  
A refundable reservation hold (₹50–₹100) signals genuine user intent. The amount is redeemable upon purchase or partially refunded if the item is unavailable, filtering casual browsing and increasing conversion likelihood.

---

### 3. Monetization Strategy
**Challenge:**  
Generating revenue without discouraging retailer participation.

**Mitigation:**  
TryLocal adopts a phased, value-first approach:
- Free onboarding during the MVP phase  
- Gradual introduction of optional subscriptions, pay-per-visit, or lead-based pricing  
- Premium analytics and visibility tools once measurable value is demonstrated

## Final Positioning
TryLocal does not replace traditional e-commerce platforms. It addresses one of their most costly shortcomings—high return rates driven by fit uncertainty and expectation gaps.

By keeping discovery online and shifting trial and purchase offline, TryLocal:
- Reduces unnecessary logistics  
- Minimizes environmental impact  
- Restores buyer confidence  
- Empowers local retailers with digital visibility and increased footfall

## Technical Details

### Frontend Architecture
- Mobile-first design using semantic HTML5 and modern CSS3  
- CSS variables for seamless dark/light theme switching  

### Dynamic UI Rendering
- Vanilla JavaScript for dynamic product grids and modal pop-ups  
- High performance without heavy frameworks  

### State Management
- Client-side filtering and sorting based on category, fabric, and price  

### External Integrations
- **WhatsApp Business API** – direct communication between users and local retailers  
- **Google Maps URL Scheme** – dynamic store location links to drive physical footfall  

### Data Structure
- Structured JSON-like array for inventory details including product name, price range, store location, and contact information
